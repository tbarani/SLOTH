# test3 — intergranular fission gas bubbles defined by the Merope mesh

Same model and thermodynamics as `test2`, but the initial gas bubbles are **regions of the
mesh**, not an analytic field placed by SLOTH. The mesh is generated by Merope and carries one
physical surface per grain (`grain_1..5`) and one per bubble (`bubble_1..4`); SLOTH only reads
those attribute sets for the initial condition (`eta_gi = 1` on `grain_i`, `eta_b = 1` on the
`bubble_j`), so the bubble positions, radii and interfaces are fixed by the committed mesh.

## From Merope to the mesh

`merope.mesh.MeshGenerator2D` (added to the Merope clone, `modules/merope_core/...`) is driven
from Python exactly like Merope's own examples:

```bash
# /home/tommy/projects/sloth_trials/merope2d/make_geo.py is the parameterized script
PYTHONPATH=/home/tommy/projects/sloth_trials/Merope/install/lib python3 \
  /home/tommy/projects/sloth_trials/merope2d/make_geo.py \
  --lx 600 --ly 600 --ngrains 5 --seed 0 \
  --bubbles 4 --bubble-radius 22 \
  --lc-coarse 25 --lc-fine 5 --dist-max 15 --out poly.geo
gmsh poly.geo -2 -format msh2 -setnumber Mesh.RecombineAll 1 -o poly.msh
```

`generate_mesh.sh` in this directory runs exactly those commands (the committed `poly.geo` and
`poly.msh` are their output, pinned by the seed and the parameters). The generator:

- computes the exact periodic Laguerre cells from the `sac_de_billes` seeds (the cell is the
  intersection of the bisector half-planes over all 9 periodic images of every other seed), so
  neighbouring cells share the same boundary lines and tile the box exactly;
- places `nbubbles` disks of radius `bubble-radius` on grain-boundary edge midpoints (farthest
  point selection, deterministic), carves them out of the grain polygons with circular arcs,
  and tags every piece of a disk `Physical Surface("bubble_j")` (a bubble straddling a grain
  boundary is several conic pieces of the same physical surface);
- writes `Physical Surface("grain_k")`, `Physical Curve("box")`, `Periodic Line` pairs on the
  box edges, and a gmsh Background Field grading the mesh from 5 nm at the grain boundaries and
  bubbles to 25 nm inside the grains.

The area is checked by the generator (grains with their holes plus bubbles = 600x600).

## What changed relative to test2

- The mesh contains the bubbles (`poly.msh` has `bubble_1..4`); the grain polygons do not
  include them.
- `main.cpp` no longer collects grain-boundary midpoints or builds a tanh bubble field: the
  bubble attribute sets are discovered from the mesh (`bubble_` prefix) and `eta_b` is
  initialized by projecting the constant 1 on their union. The initial interface is therefore
  one element wide (sharp), not the 30 nm tanh of test2.
- The `-nb` / `-r` options are gone: the bubble layout is a property of the mesh.
- Everything else (three-problem staggered model, coefficients, boundary conditions, AMR
  switches, post-processing) is identical to test2.

## Running

```bash
ctest -R "FissionGas2Dtest3"
```

The smoke run uses `-noamr -t 50 -dtmin 5 -dtmax 5`. References in `ref/` were generated from
the committed mesh with exactly this command; `op_time_specialized.csv` columns 3-4 (bubble
inventory) are compared at 1e-8 relative, the gas inventory at 1e-2.
